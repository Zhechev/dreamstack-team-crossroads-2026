# Citable Terms v1

These terms apply to every order placed through Citable. Each order and receipt records the hash of this exact text.

## 1. What you are buying
- An expert agent answers your question using only the sources its expert approved. "Expert Verified (version N)" means the expert approved the sources and rules of that agent version. It does not mean a person checked your answer.
- A real-estate answer is a **price check**. It is not an appraisal or a valuation, and it is not legal, tax or financial advice.

## 2. Citations
- Every figure in an answer cites a source unit and that unit's as-of date. Data can be out of date after its as-of date.
- Before your payment settles, Citable checks every citation automatically: the answer must be signed by the agent version you ordered, each cited unit must exist and belong to that agent, the quoted text must appear in the unit, and the as-of date must match.
- If any citation fails, your payment is not settled, the order is marked as failed, and the agent gets a negative reputation event. You pay only for answers whose citations pass.

## 3. Payment
- You approve a spending cap once per problem. During this demo the cap is moved into a problem wallet that Citable holds for you, and your assistant pays agents from it without asking again. It can never spend more than the cap.
- When the problem closes, whatever is left goes back to your wallet.
- This demo runs on Base Sepolia testnet with test USDC only. No real money moves.

## 4. Your data
- Experts never receive your identity. Agents use placeholders such as `{{full_name}}`, which only your assistant fills in.
- Citable keeps your question and the answer only to deliver the order and show your receipt. We never ask for ID numbers.
- Buyers are pseudonymous: we know a wallet address and nothing else.

## 5. Experts
- Experts warrant that they have the rights to the data they add and disclose any conflicts of interest, including their own listings.
- Only answers leave an agent, never its knowledge base.
- Citable hosts expert sources as a processor. These terms also serve as the data processing agreement.

## 6. Demo notice
- The experts and agents in this demo are fictional and labelled "Demo expert, fictional". Synthetic deal records are labelled synthetic.
- This is a hackathon prototype, provided as is.
