# Pitch Deck — 8 slides, 3 minutes

> Content for the PDF and the stage pitch. Style: [[Brand]]. Claims: [[ADR-014 Promises and claims]]. Facts and sources: [[Fact Check 2026-10-10]].

1. **Citable** — *"Ask your AI. Get an answer an expert stands behind."* Hook: €180k for a 2-bed in Lozenets — fair or not?
2. **Problem** — AI doesn't know what happened this week; nobody stands behind its answers; experts can't sell know-how to AI users because building an agent is too much work.
3. **Solution** — we build the agent, the expert approves it. Three tiers: ~0.50 automated answer → ~20 human review → 50+ consultation. *AI That Does the Work* — the expert enters only when it matters.
4. **Demo** — the 2-minute video (or stills: evaluation, one-time capped approval, cited price check).
5. **Why it's different** — "ChatGPT knows asking prices. Our agents know what actually sold last month." Every figure with source and date — and you never pay for a bad citation; Expert Verified per version; a human on call.
   **Competition 2×2** (human expert ↔ automated · subscription/access ↔ paid per answer): JustAnswer — human experts, **$28–$125/month subscription** · TollBit / Cloudflare — bots pay publishers for content access · ChatGPT / Perplexity — free, nobody stands behind it · **Citable — cited expert answers, 0.50 per answer, a human only when needed.** No lawsuit mention.
   Proof line: *"x402 is a Linux Foundation standard backed by 50+ members including Visa, Mastercard, Stripe and Google."* No volume figures.
6. **Business** — *"We take more where we do the work"*: 30% on 0.50 automated answers (we host and pay the LLM), 10% on reviews and consultations. Fee is its own transfer — funds never pass through us. Seller line: *"We build your agent. You approve it. It brings you clients."* First 5 experts: 0% on tiers 2–3 for 6 months.
7. **Who can use it** — Today: *"Works in Claude — every plan, Free included"*, testnet USDC, a hard cap you approve once. Next: top up by card, paid out in euros, crypto underneath; renovation quotes, used cars. Next also: any assistant with MCP connectors. Vision: any expert, any field, from the assistant you already use.
8. **Team & build** — Kris Georgiev, Mitko Zhechev · built with Lovable + Claude Code · open source (repo URL).
