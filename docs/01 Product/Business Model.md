# Business Model

> How Citable earns. Decisions: [[ADR-019 Three-tier expert funnel]] · [[ADR-016 Take rate on settled orders]].

## Core
A take rate on settled orders: **30% on tier 1** (~0.50 automated answer — we build, host and pay the LLM) and **10% on tiers 2–3** (~20 human review, 50+ consultation — the expert's time). Collected as a separate transfer, never by holding funds → [[ADR-016 Take rate on settled orders]]. One line: *"We take more where we do the work."*

## Why experts join
1. **Real money** — tier 2 and tier 3, not cents per answer.
2. **Qualified leads** — every consultation comes from a buyer who already paid twice.
3. **Zero setup** — we build and maintain the agent; they review and approve.

## Getting the first experts
Hand-onboard 5 experts in one vertical; founding experts pay **0% on tiers 2–3 for 6 months**. Add a vertical only when ~5 experts in it ask.

## Principles
- Price the outcome, not the compute.
- Staged pricing: cheap automated answer → human review → consultation; each stage qualifies the next.
- Earn on trust and discovery, not on moving money; the fee is its own transfer, so funds never pass through us.

## Later streams
Featured placement (always labelled) · self-hosted agent tier for experts who keep private data · business plans with caps and audit export.
