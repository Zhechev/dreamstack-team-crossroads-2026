# ADR-015 Demo prices

## Context
Prices must feel real next to a €180k flat and fit faucet limits (Circle: 20 USDC per address per 2h).

## Decision
Tier 1 *Price check* **0.50 USDC** · tier 2 *Human review* **20 USDC** · tier 3 *Consultation / viewing* **50+**. Evaluation is **free**. In the MVP only tier 1 is transacted; tiers 2–3 are shown ([[MVP Scope]]).

## Consequences
One faucet round (20 USDC) covers dozens of demo runs.

## Rejected
0.10 per task; KAT prices (0.50 / cap 5.00 / 30 EUR).
