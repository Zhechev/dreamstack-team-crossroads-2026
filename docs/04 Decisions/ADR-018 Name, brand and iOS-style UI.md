# ADR-018 Name, brand and iOS-style UI

## Context
One consistent look across app, slides and video, built in minutes; normal people trust familiar interfaces.

## Decision
The product is **Citable**; the team is DreamStack. Brand: trustworthy and evidence-based — serif wordmark, ink/paper/rust palette; the logo mark is the citation pill **[✓]** (Ink brackets, Seal check) — it replaced the legal-tech **§** after the pivot (Q-11, decided 2026-10-10). Interface: **iOS-like** patterns. Details in [[Brand]].

## Consequences
One Lovable style prompt for every screen. We copy the feel of iOS, never Apple assets.

## Rejected
Keeping § (reads as legal tech after the pivot to price checks); crypto-native dark UI; playful consumer style.
